# FitLogs 1.1.4

- Create and refresh a training dashboard in your own Google Drive from Settings.
- Always include completed workouts, imported activities, training notes and body measurements; there are no inclusion switches.
- Keep the body measurements tab visible, including when updating a previously hidden tab.
- Optionally sync automatically after workouts finish and local history changes.
- Keep each user's dashboard private to their Google account, with no developer data server or automatic sharing.
- Preserve offline logging, local backups, the Android package and original signing certificate.

Photos, body profiles, raw heart-rate samples and credentials stay outside the
Sheets dashboard. Real-phone Google consent and sync still need validation.
